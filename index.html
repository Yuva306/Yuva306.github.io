Hi again, I'm Yuva.

I've worn a few design hats, and interaction design fits me best.

I like to believe that a good design idea should outlive its context. That's how I hope to contribute to the larger design conversation.

Beyond design, I'm usually dancing or singing songs that nobody asked for. I take Sudoku far more seriously than a grown adult should, and collect hobbies faster than I finish them; board games, travel, and a camera I don't fully know how to use yet.

The closer I get to my goals, the further the goalpost seems to move. Maybe that's what’s so rewarding. So I'm still figuring things out, and I wouldn't have it any other way!
